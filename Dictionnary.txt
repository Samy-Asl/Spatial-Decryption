SPATIAL DECRYPTION — DICTIONARY / DICTIONNAIRE

The live dictionary shows the 26 letter patterns and their cell numbers.
It is generated from LETTERS in spatial-code.mjs, which also drives encoding
and decoding. See Rules.txt for uppercase letters and spaces.
